mandy brown wrote a compelling [note on *To Write Like a
Woman*](https://aworkinglibrary.com/writing/an-addiction).

In it, she claims that "all the potential horrors ascribed to a future AI are in
fact real and present today." This is the [mirror-world warping](https://pluralistic.net/2023/09/05/not-that-naomi/#if-the-naomi-be-klein-youre-doing-just-fine)
that turns today's problems into conspiracy theories, deflecting us from
addressing systemic problems by insisting we debate the existence of nonsense.

It's no wonder everyone I know is anxious about the future. We can't agree on
the present.
